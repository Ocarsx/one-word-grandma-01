Entrants send one word and nothing else: the single word their grandma would text about AI. A real word, a family in-joke, a typo she would actually make or a scrap of text-speak all count, as long as it is one word and it is what she would send. Entries are judged in private blind rounds: a judge model reads two words at a time and picks the funnier, more grandma-like one, and the word that wins the most comparisons takes the top place.

The word: *thingamajig* — the classic grandma word for a gadget she cannot name. Warm, a little baffled, instantly readable, and tied to machines: the thing she points at when the grandkids show her their new device.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the single word to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set), with a runtime self-check that the output is exactly one word.
